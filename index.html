Uncommon Types of Pizza


Why make this?

People usually tend to get plain cheese or pepperoni, which makes it easy to forget that there are so many other types of pizza. Some more odd than others.


Is it really that uncommon to order other pizzas?

35-40% of orders are for pizzas that aren't plain cheese or pepperoni


The List:

  • Anchovies
  • Eggs and Bacon
  • Wild Game (Kangaroo and Emu meat)
  • Figs and Prosciutto
